Observed (no interpretation beyond what the bytes say)
------------------------------------------------------
* Without EdDI: "EdDI: ABSENT", dispatcher and vq_scrninfo "skipped", cookie jar
  listed (12 entries on the TT), pixel part runs. In every run the screen area
  re-read after the restore equals the saved copy ("restore: VERIFIED").
* TT 4 planes: device form = word-interleaved planes; e.g. pen 1 (v_get_pixel
  pel 000F) sets bit 14 in all four plane words of row 0 (63A3 5696 4D8D 407F).
* Falcon truecolor 16 planes: device form = one 16-bit word per pixel, as BE
  words: white FFDF, black 0000, red F800, green 07C0, blue 001F (bit 5 never
  set by the VDI).
* With the Enhancer on Falcon TC: dispatcher(0) -> d0 = 00000100; vq_scrninfo
  format 2, clut 2, 16 planes, colours 0000/8000, r/g/b 5/5/5, genlock 1,
  [14] = 0, red bits 11..15, green 6..10, blue 0..4, genlock bit 5 -- these
  bit numbers match the BE-word reading of the copied pixels above. The
  Enhancer leaves CONTRL[2] and [4] unwritten (still 5A5A) and writes nothing
  beyond INTOUT[272] or into PTSOUT.
* NOT driven: the report-name argument (Hatari's autorun passes none); the
  probe on NVDI / a Nova card (only the user's TT can do that).

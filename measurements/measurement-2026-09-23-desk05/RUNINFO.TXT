0. What changed visually, and why — read this first
----------------------------------------------------
The drop-down menus now show keyboard shortcuts (`^O`, `^I`, `^S`, …) and are
three character cells wider.

That is not cosmetics added for this ticket; it is `copy_menu_items`
(`deskmain.c:1755-1813`). EmuTOS's menu strings live in RAM precisely so
`install_shortcuts` (`:1825-1858`) can write `'^'` + the letter into a reserve
of `1 + SHORTCUT_SIZE` characters at their right edge, and `deskmain` installs
`default_shortcuts[]` at startup (`:2013`, `:2023`). NewTOS's labels were
`&'static` ROM literals with no reserve, so it showed **none**. The shortcut
editor this ticket is about edits the array those strings render, so the
machinery had to come with it.

Measured box widths (serial `[desk05] BOX…`): Desk 22 cells, File 23, View 20,
Options 30. Separators are now generated at the box width (`adjust_menu`,
`:1623`) instead of being hand-written dash-run literals — `copy_menu_items`
leaves a separator out of the width measurement (`:1773`), which is why it
cannot widen the box.

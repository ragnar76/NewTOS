MEASUREMENT PROBLEMS and how they were handled (attempts <= 3 each)
  1. The IKBD injector scripts of the old runs did not apply any more:
     host_env.rs had its usb::poll sites folded into pump_devices() (INPUT01),
     3 sites -> 2 (one of them the timer-IRQ pump, which must not be injected into).
     method/inj-0c-apply-instrumentation.py, inj-keys02-apply-instrumentation.py
     (copies, assert 3 -> 2, replace the first site only) and apply_injector.py
     (H02 injector, step() now right after the poll in pump_devices()) adapted;
     scratch-kernels.sh/asid-kernels.sh point at the copies. The adapted injectors
     drive crash prompt, KBDVEC/EmuCON, TeraDesk->QED, desk3a and ppdesk as before.
     The first desk3a/ppdesk run of mode "on" ran into the stale anchor and was
     redone (batch2.sh on).
  2. Stale probe expectations after PORT09 (libc fa99b4d8: crt0 joins the desktop
     form PATH=\0C:\ in place and cuts the block at ARGV=). Probes ENVKID (MEM03,
     inside S2ALL), PPZ and PPDESK (own env / kid env == default_env) therefore
     report FAIL although the kernel delivers the right block (the AES PD's env
     printed by the same probe is intact; PPAUTO/PPACC pass). Adapted probes:
     tests/envkidj.c (checks getenv/argc/argv), tests/pparentj.c (accepts both env
     forms), built by build-ppj.sh into bin/ENVKIDJ.NAP, PPZJ.NAP, PPDESKJ.NAP, run
     by batch2.sh in all four modes. The ORIGINAL probes' FAIL stays in the logs.
  3. A host stall (the Mac throttled background processes for ~45 min in the
     strict series, caffeinate added afterwards): strict e9plain showed no boot
     progress (494 s driver timeout), strict s2all/crashtst timers fired at
     1068 s / 954 s. Rerun (batch3.sh): e9plain twice PASS, crashtst PASS with
     correct dumps (serial/a13-strict-rpi4-*-rerun*). Not reproducible, a
     host problem. The stalled crashtst dumps (a13-strict-rpi4-crashtst-t40/t70)
     are NOT valid; use the -rerun ones.

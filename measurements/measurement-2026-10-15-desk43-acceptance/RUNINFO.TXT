0. METHOD, AND WHY THERE ARE void/ RUNS
  First attempt (wall-clock screendumps, qmp_timed.py, seconds from QEMU start): the Mac is a laptop and went to sleep/woke repeatedly
  (pmset: 840 sleep/wakes since boot; battery 16 %, no AC), so 11 dumps came out minutes late (t=199 ... 2549 s instead of 25..40 s) and
  one cycle run produced black dumps. Everything of that attempt is in void/wallclock/ (acc-*/acck-* series, serial + screens) and
  void/cyc-* (first cycle run; its serial log showed 20 cycles). NOT used for any verdict below.
  Second attempt (this RUNINFO): AC power (checked with pmset -g batt after every run, line "battery:" in each "run ..." output; always
  'AC Power'), caffeinate -dimsu for the session, ONE QEMU at a time, screendumps keyed to serial markers:
    method/mrun.sh + mdrive.py: a dump is taken N seconds after the n-th match of a regex in the guest serial log (e.g.
    "[desk] desktop up", "starting C:\HELLOGEM", "TOTAL +106", "Press any key", "[tag] desktop feed" = the n-th injected byte of the
    injector script, n from method/fx.py). Marker missing within 150 s -> *.MISSING.png and rc 3 (happened only in the two runs listed
    under void/el1-twin-misskey, a mistake in my step list: desk=kernel never prints "falling back").
  Comparison by pixels (method/pixdiff.py; PNG files are never byte-equal because of the PNG header), serial by hex-normalised diff.
  Other void/ directories: probes-badtag (probes2.sh clobbered its path variable with mode.sh's P -> tags p--on...; rerun after the fix),
  el1-twin-misskey (see above, rerun), kc-app-timing (kc/km "app" dump 5 s after launch landed at the program's end on the EL1 twin; rerun
  with 3 s, identical), tdqed-f1-lost (TeraDesk -> QED: F1 5 s after launch was lost; at 15 s it works; dumps timed after the Ctrl-Q
  were mine, see 6), tdqed-aq-timing (after-qed dump 5 s late for EL0 only; rerun with 3 s, identical).
  Generic lesson: the desktop processes injected input several seconds after the injector's "feed" serial line (first Alt-C/directory
  read), so dumps right after a feed line can show an earlier state; script groups were spaced out where a state had to be shown.

A54 counter-measurement -- wind_get(WF_SCREEN) must not claim a buffer that
does not exist.

Taken 2026-09-12, after the fix in tos-api/src/aes/window.rs: the WF_SCREEN arm
of wind_get no longer writes four zero words and returns 1; it returns 0 and
writes nothing, the way wm_get's own default arm refuses a field this AES does
not implement (references/emutos/aes/gemwmlib.c:1590-1591).  The absence itself
is registered in docs/COMPATIBILITY.md.

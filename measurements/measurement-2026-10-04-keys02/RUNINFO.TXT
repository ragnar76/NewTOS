PROGRAM (sdk/examples/kbdvec.c, built with sdk/build.sh)
  appl_init; p = Kbdvbase(); old = p[-1]; check no slot is 0; p[-1] = hook
  (hook: record x0, then chain to old with x0/x1 unchanged).
  phase A: evnt_multi(MU_KEYBD) with NO timer until the RIGHT key word, then
           MU_KEYBD|MU_TIMER(20) until the hook saw 4 codes.
  phase B: Bconin(2) once, then Bconstat/evnt_timer until 6 codes.
  restore (run1 only), print, PASS/FAIL, appl_exit, Pterm.

  +600 right break 0xCD AND up break 0xC8 in the SAME poll,
  +900 'a' make 0x1E, +920 'a' break 0x9E,
  +2000.. Alt make, 'c' make, 'c' break, Alt break (desktop, program gone).
  The injector runs after every usb::poll(): pump_input and both blocking
  read loops (the KEYS01 injector froze during a blocking read).

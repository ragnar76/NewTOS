RESULT
------
Q3 (reference, screendumps/ref-hatari-falcon-screen1-1.png rows 2-7): CONFIRMED. With ESC p active EmuTOS fills
exactly one cell in the FOREGROUND colour for ESC K at odd column 11 (row 3) and at the last column 79 (row 6), and
for ESC o at even column 10 (row 4) and column 0 (row 7); ESC K at even column 10 (row 2) and ESC o at odd column 11
(row 5) erase with the background only.  This is the ascii_out(' ') border cell of erase_to_eol_impl /
erase_from_bol_impl (bios/vt52.c:471-500,752-775; blank_out's even/odd pairing, bios/conout.c:586-596).
Per the ticket's Q3 rule NewTOS erases uniformly with the background (docs/COMPATIBILITY.md, CON04).

Screen 1, NewTOS vs EmuTOS (qemu-vt52a-wt-t28.png vs ref-hatari-falcon-screen1-1.png): 4 of 2400 cells differ --
row 3 col 11, row 4 col 10, row 6 col 79, row 7 col 0 = exactly the four Q3 cells.  Everything else identical:
all sequences, swallowed escapes ("[Q]"), discarded control bytes ("<>"), BS/HT/LF/VT/FF, ESC Y clamping, ESC j/k,
ESC I/L/M/l, colours 0..15 (EmuTOS with the desktop's workstation open, NewTOS from \AUTO\ without one =
falcon_default_palette), raw Handle 5 glyphs incl. the half ESC carried across Handle 5, the cursor cell.
Q1 answered: initial wrap OFF on both (row 16 "abcg"), also for a TOS program started from the EmuTOS desktop.
Screen 2 (qemu-vt52b-wt-t28.png vs ref-hatari-falcon-screen2.png): 0 cells differ.

Cursor: after Cursconf(2) the cursor is steady -- Hatari six shots all show it, QEMU t28..t31 identical; after
Cursconf(3) it blinks -- Hatari shots 1-6 on/off/on/on/off/on (ref-hatari-falcon-blink-1/-2.png), QEMU eight shots
0.25 s apart off,on,on,off,off,on,on,off (qemu-vt52c-wt-blink-b0/-b1.png) = 0.5 s per phase (decision Q5).
So EmuTOS' Cursconf 2 = steady, 3 = blink (the code, not its comments); docs/api/xbios/15-cursconf.md corrected.

Before CON04 (qemu-vt52a-head-t28.png): every ESC printed its letter, LF did CR, BS erased -- the garbage the
ticket describes.

Regression smoke, head vs wt, 0 differing cells in each pair: regauto t28 (HELLO2 + CoreMark banner, mintlib printf
without staircase), regauto t50 (NEWDESK desktop), regtera t45 (TeraDesk desktop).  No [EXC]/PANIC in any serial log.

Not covered here (Tester / real Pi): a TOS program launched from NEWDESK/TeraDesk by click (cursor shown by
v_enter_cur / sh_toalpha, hidden again on return), EmuCON stand-in, crash report, qemu-virt board, real Pi 4.

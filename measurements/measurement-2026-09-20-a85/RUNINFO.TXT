      trailing XOR this now MATTERS for objects without an opaque fill: an unboxed G_TEXT/G_FTEXT,
      G_IBOX or G_USERDEF that is SELECTED via objc_change(redraw) shows the correct XOR bar, but a
      later objc_change(unselected, redraw) redraws its text over the still-inverted block instead

      selected state of such objects was (wrongly) white glyphs on the untouched ground, so the
      redraw-deselect happened to work. TeraDesk's SETMASK list rows are exactly this class; the
      dialog's initial draw is right (measured), a second click on another row is expected to

PROBES (method/tests): page 0 is the one VA with a different PA per process;
each process keeps a marker in a free page-0 cell (0xFF8) and checks it after
switches. ASIDSTR: A 32x Pexec child (fresh page 0 must read 0, parent cell
intact), B 4 chains of 6 generations, C inactive-ASID narrowing (a Pexec 3+4
thread Mfree's the victim's page while the victim sleeps; protection on ->
victim stopped 0xFFFF, off -> write goes through), D 200x evnt_timer(1) with
the accessory ASIDACC (checks its own page 0 on every wake, prints every 300
wakes). ASIDPERF: no C, 64/4x6/400 iterations, for timing.

CARD DEFECT (host side, not the kernel): in modes on, on-asid, off-asid,
strict-asid, on-tiny the seeded card held C:\P10\ONLYNCP.NCP with 0 bytes
(after the macOS FAT unmount -- the file had its size while mounted; reproduced
by hand), so Pexec answered EPLFMT (errno 66) and PORT10 reported 1 of 44.
rerun-port10.sh checks the card after the unmount and re-writes a short file:
PORT10 ALL PASS (44) in all five modes (serial/a4-<mode>-rpi4-port10-rerun-*).
proc02's cards did not show it; the cause in the macOS FAT driver is not
investigated (out of scope).
ORPHAN: a first series start was stopped after 20 s; one of its child
scripts ran the mode-on PPARENT run once more in parallel (timed out, its
screendump was deleted). PPARENT/ENVRN for mode on were run again afterwards
(serial/a4-on-rpi4-pparent/envrn-* are the clean reruns, PASS).

NEGATIVE CONTROLS (neg-runs.sh, deliberately broken kernels)
  neg-notlbi (ASIDs off, no TLBI in restore_boot_ttbr0, memprot=off):
    ASIDSTR FAIL -- part D 101/200 waits with a foreign page 0, ASIDACC
    bad=101/300. Parts A/B do not see it on QEMU (the child's own pool pages
    evict the page-0 entry from QEMU's soft TLB) -- part D is the QEMU-
    sensitive check.
  neg-noinval (asid=on, no per-VA invalidation): MPROT3B FAIL in case 'f'
    (write after its own Mfree goes through -- same ASID); ASIDSTR PASS --
    the inactive-ASID case (part C) is NOT observable on QEMU.

SWITCH COST (checks/swcost-a4.txt; QEMU, indicative only; CNTVCT 62.5 MHz,
counter kernel; per call of the TTBR0 primitive, UART lines between them not
included; install includes memprot::sync)
                 restore_boot_ttbr0   install_ttbr0   wall A/B/D s (product)
  on             3.76 us              1.68 us         15.17/2.87/0.40
  on-asid        4.08 us              5.84 us         11.96/2.27/0.40
  on-tiny        3.98 us              9.83 us (80 rollovers)
  off            2.08 us              1.39 us          0.33/0.06/0.40
  off-asid       2.05 us              3.50 us          0.34/0.06/0.40
  strict(-asid)  3.63 / 4.09 us       1.70 / 5.86 us  14.77 / 11.89 (A)
  On QEMU a switch with ASIDs is MORE expensive: restore_boot_ttbr0 without
  its TLBI costs the same as with it, and the install grows by ~2 us -- every
  ASID change of TTBR0 apparently costs QEMU a soft-TLB flush [Schluss, from
  these numbers and neg-noinval; QEMU source not pinned]. Part A with
  protection on is ~20 % faster with ASIDs (per-VA invalidations instead of
  vmalle1is in memprot::sync). The benefit on hardware is unmeasured.
  Baseline check: ASIDPERF on the proc02 kernel (mode on):
  A 13.8 s -- the ~200 ms per Pexec with protection on predates stage 4
  (serial/baseline-proc02kernel-on-rpi4-asidperf-*).

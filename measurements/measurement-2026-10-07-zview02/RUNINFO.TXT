RESULT IN ONE LINE
  NewTOS cut the double-click window at the application's MU_TIMER: zView
  polls evnt_multi with a 20-ms timer, count_clicks was capped at that
  deadline and returned one click per press. EmuTOS runs the window on the AES
  tick, independently of any call, extends it by 3 ticks per click and
  publishes the gesture only when it closes, to the wait parked at that
  moment. Rebuilt 1:1 (b_click/b_delay/post_button/abutton/acancel,
  gl_bpend); zView now opens folders on both double-click styles.

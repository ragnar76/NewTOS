measurement-2026-10-02-port06-keys -- PORT06 key-hold diagnosis (Doom stops walking)
Date 2026-10-02, branch desk-rebuild, HEAD 1f6dbf4, shared tree unchanged.

WHAT
  One headless QEMU raspi4b run (640x480), Doom autostarted by #Z, a scripted
  IKBD injector holds the up-arrow key for 5 s and later for 2 s. Logged:
  kernel side every key word entering / leaving the IKBD queue
  (console::push_rx / rx_pop_key) with hz_200; Doom side every MU_KEYBD event
  and every press/release the platform layer hands to DOOM (keyq_put), in
  DG_GetTicksMs (= hz_200 * 5).

WHY THE INJECTOR IS FAITHFUL TO THE PI
  The injector calls crate::ikbd::feed with 0x48 once at key-down and 0xC8
  once at key-up -- exactly what kernel/src/drivers/usb/hid.rs:69-96
  (keyboard_report) feeds for a held key: it diffs each boot report against
  the previous one and emits a make only for a NEW usage, a break only for a
  VANISHED one. A held key produces no further bytes, whether or not the
  keyboard re-sends its unchanged report. QEMU raspi4b has no USB keyboard,
  so the USB transport itself is NOT exercised here.

RESULT (serial/run1-hold-serial.log, lines after tr '\r' '\n')
  K06 Kbrate=0f02 hold_first=360 hold_repeat=100 ms
  [k06] feed 0x48 at hz_200 6006 (+3001)          key down
  [k06] push_rx 0x4800 at hz_200 6006             ONE key word queued
  [k06] rx_pop_key 0x4800 at hz_200 6006
  K06 evnt MU_KEYBD kc=4800 ks=0 at 30030 ms
  K06 put PRESS key 173 at 30030 ms
  K06 put RELEASE key 173 at 30395 ms              Doom gives up after 365 ms
  [k06] feed 0xc8 at hz_200 7005 (+4000)          key up, 5 s later: nothing queued
  [k06] feed 0x48 at hz_200 7605 (+4600)          second hold (2 s)
  [k06] push_rx 0x4800 at hz_200 7605
  K06 put PRESS key 173 at 38030 ms
  K06 put RELEASE key 173 at 38415 ms              again 385 ms
  [k06] feed 0xc8 at hz_200 8006 (+5001)
  => over a 5 s hold Doom receives exactly 1 key event and 0 repeats; it
     declares the key released 365 ms after the press (hold_first_ms).

  Serial traffic during Doom: after "[launch]" the only kernel lines are the
  [k06] instrumentation lines; no [sched]/[usb]/... lines. Doom's own stats
  line every 5 s (DOOM_STATS=5). QEMU's PL011 does not model the baud rate,
  so the cost of serial output cannot be measured headlessly.

  Screendumps: demo playback (attract mode), so the key has no visible effect
  in the picture -- the evidence is the platform-layer log, not the image.

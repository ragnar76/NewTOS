SIDE FINDING (not the crash): every vfork leaves the Pexec-5 environment
  page (0x400000, 0x401000, 0x402000) allocated until the parent ends: the
  child's Mfree of it only detaches the child (further owner, region.rs
  detach -> Kept); EmuTOS xmfree frees it. That is the 8192-byte "ST
  largest block" delta VFORKT reports (2 pages after 2 vforks).

   E  empty queue, evnt_multi(500 ms)                         expect 32           (no-acc build only: it sleeps)

   C  appl_write(self,16), appl_init again, evnt_multi         expect 16 + bytes   (the A25 attempt-2 sequence)

   G  no acc: evnt_multi(5000 ms) with nothing coming          expect 32
   G1 acc: evnt_mesag; the accessory opens+closes a full-screen window -> AES posts WM_REDRAW to us from ANOTHER process
                                                               expect 1, msg0=20, msg3=our handle
   G2 acc: evnt_multi(5000 ms); the accessory repeats the window cycle 10 ticks later
                                                               expect 16 (WM_REDRAW) -- or at least 32 after 5 s

RESULT (serial/a112-<run>-serial.log; memprot on and off identical):
   noacc-on / noacc-off: E A B C D F G all PASS, "A112: RESULT PASS", program exited rc=0.
       -> an already-queued message (self-sent via appl_write, or queued by the AES during our own wind_open) IS seen by
          evnt_multi(MU_MESAG) on entry; evnt_mesag too; a second appl_init in between changes nothing; FIFO order kept.
   acc-on / acc-off: A B C D F G1 PASS. G2: "A112: G2 entering evnt_multi(MU_MESAG|MU_TIMER, 5000)", then the accessory's
       "A112ACC: cycle 2: own window 2 opened+closed" (the WM_REDRAW for our window is posted), then NOTHING from the program for
       the rest of the 40 s run: neither the message nor the 5000 ms timer ends the evnt_multi. (The accessory's own
       "A112ACC: message 20 handle 2" is the WM_REDRAW its own wind_open queued for its window 2 -- expected.)
       -> DEFECT: a program waiting in evnt_multi is woken neither by a message posted by another process nor by its own
          MU_TIMER deadline once another AES process ran during the wait.

HISTORY (honest record): serial/run1-first-revision/ = first probe revision (no -DA112_ACC variant; the same program on both cards,
   binaries since rebuilt, not kept). noacc runs: identical PASS. acc runs: the program printed "A112: init ap_id=0" and then
   hung in case E (pure evnt_multi(MU_MESAG|MU_TIMER, 500) on an empty queue) while the accessory (evnt_timer loop) ran:
   "[sched] switch -> slot 0" and nothing more from the program. Same defect, timer-only form: a 500 ms timer never expires
   while a peer is runnable. The revision was then split so the acc card skips E and reaches G1/G2.

A25 EARLIER OBSERVATION: measurement-2026-10-17-a25/serial/attempt2-evnt_multi-observation/*.log print
   "evnt_multi events=32 (MU_MESAG=1 MU_TIMER=32)": that probe revision used MU_MESAG = 1. 1 is MU_KEYBD
   (tos-types/src/aes.rs:367; EmuTOS include/aesdefs.h:256); MU_MESAG is 0x0010 (aes.rs:375; aesdefs.h:260). It waited for
   MU_KEYBD|MU_TIMER, so "timer only" was the correct answer. PROBE ERROR, not an AES defect (case A/C here, with 0x0010, PASS).

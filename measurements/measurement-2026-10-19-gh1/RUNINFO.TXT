RESULTS
 1 Reproduced, both kernels, plain builds: screendumps/head-plain-popup.png, pre-plain-popup.png: after the click on the popup
   button the C:\ACC\ window frame (title bar, info line, scroll bars, sizer) is drawn over the dialog; it stays after Esc
   (head-plain-esc.png) until the dialog closes. Window work area NOT painted in QEMU (the photo shows it white: not reproduced).
   0 EXC/PANIC in all runs.
 2 Sequence (serial/head-log.log, lines with [gh1]):
   a) menu press (+500) -> AC_OPEN posted at once; the accessory runs wind_update(BEG_UPDATE), form_center, form_dial, objc_draw
      and form_do's fm_own(TRUE) BEFORE the release (+510): no "set_mown -> 0" -> the mouse owner stays the desktop (id 1).
   b) popup-button press (+1600): "post_button new=1 completes wait #0 (mowner_pd=1)" AND "... wait #1" -- the accessory's and
      NEWDESK's parked button waits are both completed.
   c) form_do returns, NEWDESK's evnt_multi returns MU_BUTTON (480,250, bret 1) -- no wind_update(BEG_UPDATE) by NEWDESK --
      wind_find, wind_get, wind_set(1, WF_INFO) -> "frame h=1 top=true pc=None" = the whole frame of window 1 repainted over the
      dialog, while the accessory holds the screen lock (tas=2) and is inside BEG_MCTRL.
   d) second form_do (after the popup, owner correctly = accessory, mowner_pd=0): the Cancel press again completes wait #0 AND
      wait #1 -> NEWDESK gets the Cancel click (468,455) as well.
 3 A112 is not the trigger: pre-A112 (f34f9306) shows the same delivery (serial/pre-log.log: NEWDESK evnt_multi MU_BUTTON 480,250,
   wind_set(1,WF_INFO), frame pc=None) and the same picture (pre-plain-popup.png; the frame there also covers the popup box,
   because NEWDESK ran after the popup was drawn).
 4 Experiment own (serial/head-own.log, screendumps/head-own-popup.png): owner-only post_button alone does NOT fix it: the popup
   press now completes only wait #1 = NEWDESK (owner still the desktop from 2a); the accessory's form_do still returns (the
   in-kernel form_do loop does not use the parked wait), NEWDESK still draws the frame. The Cancel press (owner = accessory)
   completes only wait #0 there.
 5 Double-click variant (serial/head-dbl.log): the presses complete NEWDESK's wait too (wait #1 at +1600), but NEWDESK does not run
   while the accessory sits in the in-kernel form_do; the completed wait is only returned after form_do ends.

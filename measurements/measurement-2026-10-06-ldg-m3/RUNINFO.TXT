FINDINGS (open; no kernel change made -- STOP rule of the task)
  B1 kernel AES: evnt_multi/evnt_button return EV_MB = the live button
     state after the double-click window (tos-api/src/aes/event.rs fill_rets
     -> vdi::mouse_buttons()). EmuTOS reports the event's state: b_delay
     forks bchange(gl_bdesired, gl_bclick) (aes/geminput.c:155-180), bchange
     sets button = new and posts it (:328-345), ev_rets returns button or, after a second
     transition, pr_button (aes/gemevlib.c:42-58) -- i.e. "down" for a
     click; docs/api/aes/event.md:259-266 states the same. Effect: WinDom
     drops every normal click on forms/toolbars/window menus
     (evnt_windom.c:334 `mwin && evnt.mbut & 0x01`).
  B2 LDG module heap (libc + module model, docs/sdk/10-ldg-modules.md): the
     module's exit-time stdio close leaves its malloc free list pointing into
     a hunk its own Pterm frees; every later malloc inside the module (called
     by the client) uses freed memory. Blocks POPUP.NLD here and every zView
     codec in M4. Fix location undecided (libc / libldg / kernel ownership).
  O1 (cosmetic, kernel AES): objc_edit's caret ignores te_just -- EmuTOS
     pxl_rect applies gr_just (aes/gemobed.c:180-182); NewTOS edit_field_t
     draws at ox + find_pos*8 (tos-api/src/aes/object.rs). Visible in
     probe-on-t35.png / m3c-*-S06: caret 18 px left in the centred field.

TIMELINE point 2 (Doom ms = DOOM's own clock; sequence offsets relative to the first make; injected
times from inject.log)
 event            injected   TOS3.06 Doom   EmuTOS Doom      (Doom clock at UP make: 95870 / 16090)
 UP   PRESS         0.0        0.0 (95870)    0.0 (16090)
 RIGHT PRESS        1.5        1.505          1.465
 RIGHT RELEASE      3.0        3.020          2.960
 UP   RELEASE       5.0        4.960          5.070
 -> UP reported held from make to its own break (no release at RIGHT make/break), RIGHT exactly
    1515 / 1495 ms (injected 1500). Doom sees every byte (K02 byte) then one put per byte.
 LEFT tap 100 ms (inj.)  PRESS+RELEASE 50 ms apart (TOS3.06) / 55 ms (EmuTOS)
 LEFT tap  40 ms (inj.)  55 ms (TOS3.06) / 50 ms (EmuTOS)   -> tap = press+release pair, never merged
   or dropped; length resolution here ~50 ms = Doom poll rate under Hatari (100 vs 40 ms injected are
   not distinguishable). F10 tap (50 ms injected) 170-175 ms, y tap 25-55 ms.
 Make and break of the SAME tap arrive in order (every PRESS followed by RELEASE).
 -nokbdvec fallback (same sequence): UP PRESS 0.0, RIGHT PRESS 1.45, UP RELEASE 1.585 (= RIGHT +135 ms,
   while UP still held: the heuristic's known defect), RIGHT RELEASE 3.125, no UP press after;
   taps last 370/410 ms (both). EmuTOS the same (UP released at RIGHT +30 ms, taps ~400-460 ms).
   -> the kbdvec route fixes exactly what the fallback gets wrong.

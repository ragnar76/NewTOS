RESULT: PARTIAL. Design steps 1-3 implemented and checked; STOPPED before steps 4-6 (and the step-9 docs)
because the design's table inventory (§2.4, "genau zwei" tables coupled to MD_BLOCK_COUNT) is incomplete:
memprot.rs has two more tables sized MAX_RECORDS (= MD_BLOCK_COUNT*3+2 = 260):
  * SNAP     memprot.rs:929-932 -- the sorted snapshot of ALL allocated blocks, rebuilt on every sync
             (sync_now :732, structure_delta :1263). snapshot() stops silently at 260 blocks (:947
             `*n < buf.len()`): once the MD supply grows past 258 MDs, blocks beyond 260 vanish from the
             snapshot and refresh() writes descriptor 0 / the wrong rights for their pages (memprot on).
             It must grow with the live blocks; 72 bytes per block (start, end, mode, inherit, own,
             4 further owners, owner mask), i.e. more than RECS (16 B) and the MDs (~35 B) together.
  * VSCRATCH memprot.rs:1641-1644 -- per-2-MiB-region scratch of verify_now (:1738 `nb < blks.len()`).
             Bounded by the pages of one region (512) with the page grain memprot uses; a fixed 512
             removes the coupling (no decision needed).
Open decision for SNAP (budget/heap): see TASK/MEM05-A-STATUS-UNFINISHED.MD.

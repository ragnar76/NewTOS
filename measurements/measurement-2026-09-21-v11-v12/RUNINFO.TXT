NOT DONE / OPEN
  * Real Pi 4: not flashed (lead/user only). The VideoCore's treatment of the top byte at depth
    32 is documented in none of the in-tree Track-B sources (docs/hardware/mailbox-framebuffer.md,
    references/circle); no alpha-mode tag (0x0004_8007) is sent. Today's kernel wrote X = 0xFF on
    every pixel and displays on the Pi; after the change pen-drawn pixels carry X = 0x00 and
    cleared/complemented ones X = 0xFF. If the Pi shows anything other than the QEMU picture, the
    documented remedy is one extra tag in the allocate message: set alpha mode = 2 (ignored).
  * Consequences (2) vro_cpyfm Screen->Memory over a cleared area and (3) v_contourfill seed mode on
    the cleared background follow from the same two conversion functions but have no case in
    vdi-all.tsv / vdi-raster.tsv; not measured on a device (host: raster.rs paths go through
    device_to_rgb565/rgb565_to_device, overlay_bit.rs pins the pair).

DESIGN (short; reasons in the spec)
  * One cache UNDER every access to the card: tos-api/src/bios/bcache.rs,
    bcache::device() wraps Host::block(); the five callers in tos-api
    (Rwabs, MBR scan, Getbpb x2, the BDOS buffer lists incl. the fsio direct
    transfers) go through it. Key = absolute device LBA, so logical and
    physical Rwabs share one copy. (FreeMiNT sits above Rwabs and lets a
    program's Rwabs pass by -- named deviation.)
  * Unit 512 B (FreeMiNT CHUNK_SIZE), exact fetch of each miss run with ONE
    device request (bio_l_read), NO read-ahead (bio_pre_read is empty in
    FreeMiNT), exact LRU.
  * Linear-read share 5 % (FreeMiNT DEFAULT_PERC): a miss run is inserted
    only if run*100 <= pct*capacity.
  * Write-through: device first; a confirmed write <= 32 KiB updates/inserts
    its sectors (bio_small_write), a larger one drops the range first
    (bio_large_write), a failed one drops the range.
  * Invalidate: bios::mount() (new partition scan). Mediach is constant 0
    (non-removable card); Getbpb does not invalidate (as FreeMiNT).
  * Memory: own range at the bottom of the OS reserve at the top of DRAM
    (layout.rs block_cache), outside ST-RAM/TT-RAM, below heap/framebuffer
    (those keep their addresses), 2-MiB steps, at most half of TT-RAM;
    board-rpi4 only. Default 32 MiB (FreeMiNT 128 KB ~ 3 % of a 4-MB ST ->
    ~30 MiB of the ~950 MiB NewTOS manages on a Pi 4). Boot arguments
    bcache=<KiB> (0 = off) and bcachepct=<0..100>; boot line "[bcache] ...".

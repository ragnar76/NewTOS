Machine: QEMU `-M raspi4b`, 640x480, `-display none`, serial to file, QMP
screendumps. Card image: `tools/make-sd-image.sh` (64 MiB, MBR + FAT16), a
fresh copy per run. Interactive runs use the scratch-copy IKBD injector
(memory `desktop-interactive-verification-real-hw`):
`../measurement-2026-09-24-desk01-desk02/method/make_scratch_desk0102.sh`

the DESK10 anchor patch + the DESK08/09 injector patches. The pointer is
parked bottom-right (`parkbr`) before every screendump so the cursor never
pollutes a comparison.

`method/*.actions` are the four action lists; `method/*_script.rs` the
generated injector tables (`gen_script.py ... SCRIPT_DESK`, plus an empty
`SCRIPT_TERA`, which is what `a78_patch2.py` expects).
No `*.img` (the ticket's rule).

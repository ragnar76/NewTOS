OPEN / NOTES
  - Fwrite still writes the cache back at the end of every call (HW03: a
    device error becomes that call's EWRITF). EmuTOS writes back only on
    eviction, usrio overlap and ixclose. Kept deliberately (spec _state.md
    §3a); it costs one FAT write-back (both copies) per Fwrite that
    allocated, plus a partial record.
  - 8 buffers per list instead of EmuTOS' 2 (count is not contract; named in
    _state.md §3).
  - A whole-record transfer into a user buffer that starts in page 0 and runs
    past it is refused by Space::kptr (as for BIOS Rwabs) and ends the call
    as a user fault; before, Fread copied byte-wise through page 0. No program
    known to Fread across the page-0 boundary.
  - The directory reader (dir.rs dir_nth_sector) still walks a sub-
    directory's chain from its start for every sector -- outside this task.
  - recsiz != 512 is still unsupported by the FS layer as a whole (cache and
    LBA arithmetic assume 512-byte records), unchanged.

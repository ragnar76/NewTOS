WHAT CHANGED (method/spacing-change.diff)
  New header directive (before the first "# " slide, like @footer/@log):
    @spacing N   extra space after each item (bullet, sub-bullet, text line) in
                 twelfths of that item's character height; 0..24 (more = 24,
                 no number = ignored). Default 4 = the old fixed (ch*k)/3.
  items_block(): y += (ch*k*item_space)/12 (floor(4x/12) == floor(x/3), so the
  default is bit-identical). Lines wrapped inside one item are NOT spread:
  they keep lineh() = ch*k + ch*k/8. Decision from the dumps: with 8/10 the
  wrapped bullets of slide 2 read as one block only because their inner
  distance stays tight while the item distance grows (spacing8-slide-02.png,
  spacing10-slide-02.png).

  symbols; PRESENT.NAP sha256 98043d74...b7e14c (checks/inputs.sha256).

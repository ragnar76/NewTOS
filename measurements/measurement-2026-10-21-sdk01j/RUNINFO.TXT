ANALYSIS (does upstream mintlib do binary128?)  No. mintlib-0.60.1's long double code is the m68k 96-bit extended format only:
  include/ieee754.h union ieee854_long_double (32+32-bit mantissa with explicit integer bit, 16-bit pad), gmp/ldbl2mpn.c and
  gmp/mpn2ldbl.c (64-bit significand), stdlib/strtold.c (m68k DENORM_EXP, 80-bit NaN payload), stdio/printf_fphex.c (asserts
  sizeof(long double) == 12), mintlib/s_isinfl.c, s_isnanl.c (80-bit word layout). printf_fp.c/strtod.c/fpioconst.c and the
  q*cvt family are generic (LDBL_MANT_DIG; qefgcvt knows 113 -> 36 digits). Size of the binary128 port: 9 overlay files, glibc's
  ldbl-128 code where mintlib has the m68k one -- not a rewrite, so implemented (no STOP).

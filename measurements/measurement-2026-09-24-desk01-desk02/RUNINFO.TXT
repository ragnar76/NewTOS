/`key_step_patch.py`/`tree_dump_patch.py`. The pointer is parked bottom-right
(`parkbr`) before every screendump so the cursor never pollutes a comparison.

4. A defect the measurement found
---------------------------------
screendumps/probe-C-prefs-BEFORE-idt-fix.png is the first probe run. It shows
**DMY** pre-selected on a machine whose text view prints MDY: the `Idt`
fallback had been written twice — once in `fmt_date` (-> Mdy) and once in the
dialog's `!= DateFmt::Mdy` pre-selection (-> the second button, i.e. Dmy) —
and the two disagreed. Fixed by `model::TimeFmt::rendered()` /
`DateFmt::rendered()`, one rule that both readers go through;
screendumps/prefs-p-C-prefs.png is the same run after the fix.

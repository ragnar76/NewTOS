A37 counter-measurement -- fix_tedinfo_std: recompute te_txtlen/te_tmplen.

Taken 2026-09-12 after the fix in tos-api/src/aes/resource.rs and
tos-api/src/aes/object.rs:

  (a) new `fix_tedinfo_std` / `fix_tedinfo_std_ilp32`
      (EmuTOS aes/gemrslib.c:655-669, called from rs_readit :798):
      after the offsets have become pointers, every relocated TEDINFO's
      te_txtlen = strlen(te_ptext)+1 and te_tmplen = strlen(te_ptmplt)+1.
      Called from `relocate_and_register_core` only -- the rs_readit path.
      `relocate_blob` (the embedded GEM.RSC, EmuTOS `gem_rsc_init`,
      aes/gem_rsc.c) deliberately does NOT run it: `erd` reserves the full
      te_txtlen bytes per TEDINFO there (tools/erd.c:3007).
  (b) `ins_char` clamps a negative insert position (aes/object.rs).
      te_txtlen == 1 drives `ob_edit`'s field-full branch (gemobed.c:407-413)
      to idx == -1.  EmuTOS edits a scratch copy (D.g_rawstr, gemobed.c:349)
      and only then does strcpy(te_ptext, D.g_rawstr) (:438), so its str[-1]
      store is invisible; NewTOS edits te_ptext in place, where the same index
      would write one byte BEFORE the resource buffer.  Clamping reproduces
      EmuTOS' observable result (character dropped, field unchanged).

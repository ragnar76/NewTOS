Developer run (not the Tester's verdict). QEMU raspi4b, -append "serial=on[ ...]", memprot default (on);
one QEMU at a time, host macOS. No SD card, Pi or TT touched. Scratch (kernels, cards, targets, sockets) in

WHAT / WHY
  INPUT01: the mouse pointer froze whenever no program waited in the AES -- the USB-HID mouse was drained
  only in the trap-side pump (Host::pump_input from AES waits, the blocking console reads, Bconstat), and
  IRQs are masked inside every trap. EmuTOS moves the pointer from interrupts (bios/aciavecs.S:249 ->
  mouse_int, vdi/vdi_mouse.c:602,668-686 vb_draw) and its GEMDOS trap does not mask them (bdos/rwa.S:158-204).
  Change (kernel/src/input_gate.rs has the full reasoning): the 200 Hz timer IRQ, when it interrupted a
  program at EL0 (no trap in progress, no upcall in flight, AES fiber idle), drains ONE USB report like a
  trap-side pump; a report that is pure pointer motion (relative-mouse packets at the unchanged button
  state) is decoded at once -- cursor moves, AES user_mot/mchange runs -- anything else (button change,
  wheel, key make/break) is HELD and the IRQ drains nothing more until the next trap-side pump decodes the
  held report in place of a USB drain. So clicks, double-click window, wheel, keys, key repeat and kbdvec
  delivery keep their pre-INPUT01 moment; only the pointer moves earlier. No IRQs are opened inside
  kernel work and no pump runs inside long traps (Pexec, SD reads): a timer IRQ that fell due during a trap
  is taken on the first EL0 instruction after it, so the pointer stands still for at most one trap.
  Boot argument input=aes = exactly the old behaviour (no IRQ pump at all).

NOTES / LIMITS
  - The evidence and regression kernels were built before three text-only edits (doc comments in
    bootargs.rs/input_gate.rs/lib.rs and the wording of the "[input]" boot line); the package kernel and
    the static checks are after them.
  - QEMU raspi4b has no USB (no PCIe): the input source in every run is the scratch injector, which enters
    exactly where usb::poll's reports enter (ikbd::feed inside the pump / the IRQ capture). The real USB
    path (xHCI poll from the timer IRQ on the Pi) is HW-only. The timer PPI itself is delivered on the
    Pi (FSBENCH timed with _hz_200 there, measurement-2026-10-07-fs08-pi).
  - QEMU times are only indicative; on the Pi a Pexec of a codec reads its sectors from the card, so the
    longest single trap (= the longest remaining stand-still) may be longer than QEMU's 130 ms.
  - Not changed, measured: stand-stills inside one trap (Pexec, a long AES/VDI call such as the 85 ms
    one in zView's picture window) and while the built-in desktop (EL1 code) works itself.

S2ALL TSR under protection ON
  TSRPAR's check "TPA beyond blkln back in the pool (< 64 KiB kept)" compares
  the largest TT block before and after: ON 0x39f78000 -> 0x39b76000
  (-4 MiB-8 KiB), 3a -0x2A60. Every functional TSR check passes (resident
  code called twice, its BSS kept, its Malloc block kept, Mfree of its blocks
  EIMBA, a later program calls it). Cause: the loader allocates the TPA
  BEFORE the environment (EmuTOS: environment first, bdos/proc.c:281-292);
  with the 16-byte grain the environment and TSRRES' small block fell into the
  TPA's front alignment slack below the TPA, with the page grain (E16) there
  is no slack, they sit right after the TPA, and the freed TPA tail is a
  separate hole. D7 (Ptermres selftest, TT used bytes) PASSES under ON: the
  tail does go back to the pool. Not changed (out of scope, changes OFF
  layout too); reported.

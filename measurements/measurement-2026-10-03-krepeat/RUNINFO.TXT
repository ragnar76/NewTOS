measurement-2026-10-03-krepeat -- KEYS01 key auto-repeat, before/after
Date 2026-10-03, branch desk-rebuild, base HEAD 3387f75, KEYS01 uncommitted.

WHAT
  Two headless QEMU raspi4b runs (640x480, -append serial=on via
  tools/qemu-serial-on), Doom autostarted by #Z, the same scripted IKBD
  injector in both scratch kernels:
    before = HEAD 3387f75 (git archive) + instrumentation
    after  = working tree with KEYS01 + the same instrumentation
  Doom binary identical in both runs: the K06-instrumented DOOM.NAP of the
  PORT06 diagnosis (measurement-2026-10-02-port06-keys), so only the kernel
  differs.

INJECTOR (method/apply-instrumentation.py; scratch copies only)
  Feeds exactly what drivers/usb/hid.rs feeds for a key that goes down and
  later up: ONE make, ONE break (a boot keyboard reports changes only).
  It sits in pump_input right after usb::poll() -- i.e. in the "after" kernel
  before ikbd::poll_repeat, the same place the real USB input arrives.
  Schedule (200 Hz ticks after the first pump with an EL0 program):
    +3000/+3200/+3400/+3600  Return make, break 10 ticks later
                             (Doom: menu, New Game, episode, skill -> E1M1)
    +4200 .. +5200           hold up-arrow 5 s
    +5800 .. +6200           hold up-arrow 2 s
  console.rs logs every key word into (push_rx) and out of (rx_pop_key) the
  queue with hz_200. Doom's K06 lines log MU_KEYBD events and the
  press/release it hands to the engine.

  Late-poll case, seen by accident in both runs: the 4th Return (break
  scheduled +3610) was only fed at +3663 because Doom was loading E1M1 and
  did not pump for ~300 ms. In the "after" run a repeat would have been due at
  ~+3659, but the poll that saw it decoded the break first: Return words = 4
  (no repeat, no ghost) -- the ordering the polled design relies on.

  Screendumps (screendumps/sheet-*-t035-t043.png, 9 dumps 35..43 s):
    before: the player moves a little between t036 and t037 (~0.37 s of
            walking) and then stands still t037..t043 while the key is held.
    after:  the player walks from the start through the hall (t037, t038)
            and is against a wall from t039 on; the press is held by Doom
            until 41.1 s (log above). During the 2 s hold the player is
            still at that wall, so t047..t050 do not change -- expected.
  The dumps are wall-clock timed; key times are hz_200 (~5 ms ticks) --
  hz_200 * 5 ms matches the dump second to within the QEMU start-up offset
  (the injector armed at hz 3005 = 15 s in both runs).

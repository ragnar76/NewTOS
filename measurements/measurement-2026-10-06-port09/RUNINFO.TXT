PROBE ADAPTATIONS (method/tests, copies; the old run dirs are untouched)
  envkid.c (MEM03, in S2ALL) and pparent.c (PPARENT Z/Z-KID) read the RAW
  p_env block in main(). With mintlib's start-up that block is rewritten
  before main() (cut at ARGV=; "PATH=\0C:\\\0" joined), exactly as on TOS
  with mintlib; the kernel's delivery is unchanged. Unchanged probes would
  therefore FAIL by design: envkid "ARGV= arrived"/"argument with blanks
  arrived", pparent "own env = default_env" and "kid: env = default_env".
  The checks now test the same delivery through what the libc made of it
  (ARGV= consumed + argv[0]/argv[2]; raw block = the joined form + environ
  = {PATH=X:\}). The AES PD / initial_basepage / AUTO / accessory raw-block
  checks are unchanged (no libc walk touches those blocks).

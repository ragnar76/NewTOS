RESULTS (QEMU; counts deterministic, both zview runs identical; times NOT
representative of the Pi)
  zView BIG.JPG: 2222 Fread of exactly 1024 B (mintlib stdio, st_blksize=1024),
    6599 sectors for 4441 data sectors (+49 %), 615 495 FAT chain-walk steps.
  zView start: one input gap 1487 ms (run 2: 1504) covering the 30 Pexec calls
    of the 10 codecs, 2753 sectors; no other gap > 200 ms.
  Bench 4 MiB: sectors 10052 / 9745 / 8833 / 8705 for 512 B / 4 KiB / 64 KiB /
    one call (data = 8192); chain-walk steps 4 746 240 = exact closed-form sum.
  IRQs during the whole zView run: timer only (uart 0, other 0).
  Driver: CONTROL0=0x00000f00 (1-bit, no HS), CONTROL1 div=2 -> 13 MHz (QEMU).

measurement-2026-10-16-desk62 -- DESK62 phase 1: reproduce "hourglass stays after dropping a file on a desktop shortcut" in QEMU and
establish the call path. Analysis: docs/analysis/desk62-hourglass-after-drop-on-shortcut-2026-10-16.md.

Base: main 02288f2d, shared tree clean; nothing in the product changed. Host macOS, QEMU -M raspi4b, -append serial=on, -display none,
QMP screendumps keyed to serial markers (method/mdrive.py, copied unchanged from measurement-2026-10-16-desk43-u10-test). One QEMU at a time.
No SD card, no Pi, nothing under /Volumes/BOOT* or NEWTOS*. Scratch (tree copy, base card, per-run clones) in the session scratchpad
.../scratchpad/d62, never /tmp; per-run cards = APFS clones (cp -c) of one base image, deleted after each run; base + tree deleted at the end.

   BUG PATH ds (drop on the desktop shortcut) -- serial/ds-serial.log:
     the same sequence up to shel_write(SHW_EXEC) and graf_growbox (the "launch animation"), then wind_set x2, men_update, evnt_multi (25):

   void/ds-2-dragging.png: dump 0.3 s after the last drag step already shows the hourglass (release 0.35 s later -- timing, not a finding).

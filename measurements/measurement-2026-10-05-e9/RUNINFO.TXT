under ON/STRICT, PASS under OFF. ON: TT largest before 0x3a39a000 after
0x3a385000 (0x15000). TSRRES (PD 0x01a67000, blkln 0x2760) keeps image +
the 64-KiB MINKEEP stack after its crtinit Mshrink; its Mxalloc(64) lands
right behind that (first fit); Ptermres(blkln) frees [PD+0x3000, PD+0x13000)
-- 64 KiB, exactly the crtinit stack -- as a free block of its own between

passes because with the 16-byte grain TSRRES' small blocks fit holes below.

RESULTS (QEMU; times relative only, counts and proportions informative)
  zView start (hd seg 2): EL1 work 1607 ms of which memprot sync 1381 ms
    (refresh over 17.4 M pages); 30 Pexec (10 codecs x mode 5/3/4) 1321 ms
    + 11 Mshrink 260 ms, memprot inside 1069 + 259 ms; pointer frozen
    1589 ms in one gap. TPA = largest block ~916 MB; zero phase 0 ms (67-80
    KB zeroed, PF_FASTLOAD) -- the TPA-zeroing suspicion is refuted.
  Pexec during browsing: 0. Codecs are loaded once at start.
  Clicks: double click -> MU_BUTTON 260-275 ms, single click 200-214 ms,
    window gadget (arrow/closer) -> MU_MESAG 200-215 ms, every time.
  Thumbnails (hd seg 5, first visit): 8 photos -> 8 gaps of 154-172 ms,
    each EL0 ~63 ms + Fread ~100 ms; Fread total 784 ms/560 calls (QEMU
    SD). Second visit (seg 13): EL0 513 ms (zView decodes again, no
    thumbnail cache), Fread 4 ms (FS09 block cache).
  Drawing at 1920x1080: fill_pattern 127 M px 189 ms, dcache clean 148 MB
    43 ms over the whole hd run; per folder view ~46-48 M px of v_gdp.
    Image window x01: vro_cpyfm 1.86 M px 11.6 ms. Scroll (vga seg 7, 4
    arrows): EL1 work 18 ms total.
  evnt_multi: ~0.1-0.25 ms work per return (zView polls every 20 ms);
    3 isolated returns with 34-45 ms "work", px=0, around gadget clicks --
    not resolved (probably a wait loop the probe does not recognise).

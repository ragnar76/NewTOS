A83 PRE-CHECK RESULT (the STOP; full diagnosis in TASK/A83-STATUS-UNFINISHED.MD)
  (a) NewTOS has NO single address space: per-process TTBR0 (kernel/src/arch/aarch64/el0.rs:45-47,
      tos-api/src/bdos/process/mmu.rs:381 install_ttbr0, exceptions.rs:1486-1502 child install,
      :988-990 parent restore, :973 "both TPAs map at VA 0x100"). NEWDESK_TREE (window.rs:1070,
      draw_desk_tree :1281) reads its EL0 pointer raw at composite time regardless of the current
      process -- the same hazard, pre-existing, not this ticket. A raw EL0 read is only valid while the
      owner is current; frames ARE rebuilt from another process's AES call (nested Pexec child over the
      parent's windows; desktop_resume -> redraw_desktop -> form_dial(FMD_FINISH) over a dead child's
      windows under the boot tables). The lead's rule for exactly this case: say so and STOP.
      Proposal: keep the owner slot in Window and read through usermem::resolve_kva(slot, va)
      (pattern: xbios/screen.rs::caller_base_to_kva), kernel pointers direct, NULL/unresolvable -> "".
  (b) Windows survive their owner: pterm.rs:142-239 touches resources/pool/pd, never the AES window
      table; wm_new runs only in the shell-loop arm (shell_loop.rs:327-331), not on the kernel desktop's
      double-click path (desk/main.rs:524 run_gem_app -> launch.rs:66, gap G1/G2) nor for a nested
      EL0 Pexec child. Ticket for the lead.
  Kernel desktop passes STACK buffers as WF_NAME/WF_INFO (desk/main.rs:1108,1134,1626,1630,1701,1705);
      EmuTOS keeps w_name/w_info in the WNODE (deskwin.h:73,79); our WNode has w_name/w_info: String
      unused (desk/win.rs:101-103). Must move into the WNode when A83 lands.

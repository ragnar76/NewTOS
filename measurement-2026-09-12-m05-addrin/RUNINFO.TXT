THE POINTER WALL THAT REMAINS
  * A measured case can only carry a pointer in addr_in.  The classic binding
    packs it into the int_in WORDS, whose footprint is two WORDs on the m68k
    and four on AArch64 -- and int_in is recorded.  Such a plant is therefore
    a PRELUDE-only form (which is how case 102's title gets set).
  * The 120 byte AES record has no room for a buffer's CONTENTS.  A call whose
    only effect is what it wrote into the buffer is still measurable only
    through its return code.  Recording the buffer means a bigger record, and
    a bigger record invalidates every dump already taken.
  * Nothing here changes what the opcode guard refuses (core/cases.c).  The
    only guard arm the buffer re-opens is WF_NAME / WF_INFO.

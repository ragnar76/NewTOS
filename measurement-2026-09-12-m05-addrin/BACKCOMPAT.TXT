M05 -- the proof that nothing already measured moved.  2026-09-12.

Every dump ever taken is bound to its case table by the table's CRC32, which
the dump header carries.  A format change that made an existing table parse
differently, or that changed one byte of the records a re-run produces, would
invalidate the lot -- including the irreplaceable real-TT references under
tosdump/reference/ and the r4 measurement of this morning.  So the change was
built to be invisible to a table that does not ask for it, and that claim is
checked here three ways.

M06 -- the proof that nothing already measured moved.  2026-09-13.

Every dump ever taken is bound to its case table by the table's CRC32, which
the dump header carries.  A change that made an existing table parse
differently, or that changed one byte of the records a re-run produces, would
invalidate the lot -- including the irreplaceable real-TT references under
tosdump/reference/.  M06 moves a REFUSAL from the loader to the machine; the
claim is that nothing which was accepted before is accepted differently now,
and that the bytes are unchanged.  Checked four ways.

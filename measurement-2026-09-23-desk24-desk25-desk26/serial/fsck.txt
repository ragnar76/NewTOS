The two `rc=133` lines, and why they are NOT these three tickets
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
`d25-yes.img` and `d25-before.img` are the only two images on which a WHOLE
DRIVE was emptied (DESK25's "Yes", and the same gesture on the HEAD build
which asks nothing).  On both, `fsck_msdos -n` dies with exit 133 and prints
nothing at all -- not even its `** /dev/rdiskNs1` banner.

  * It reproduces IDENTICALLY on the HEAD build (`d25-before.img`), so it is
    not caused by DESK25: DESK25 only adds a question in front of the same
    delete.
  * The filesystem itself is consistent: both FAT copies are byte-identical
    and every cluster is free (`method/lfn_orphan_probe.py`, and the FAT dump
    in the report).
  * What is left behind are 35 ORPHAN long-filename slots: GEMDOS
    `Fdelete`/`Ddelete` erase the 8.3 entry (first byte 0xE5) and leave the
    preceding `attr == 0x0F` LFN slots untouched -- classic TOS has no long
    names, so nothing ever erased them.  The card carries them because macOS
    wrote `.fseventsd`, `.Spotlight-V100` and every `._*` companion with an
    LFN chain.
  * Proof that this is the cause: `ctl-nolfn.img` is a COPY of `d25-yes.img`
    with exactly those 35 slots marked 0xE5 (`lfn_orphan_probe.py --fix`), and
    it passes Phase 1/2/3 with 0 files, rc=0 -- last block above.
  * A single hand-made orphan chain on an otherwise live card only produces a
    WARNING ("Invalid long filename entry for /.Spotlight-V100"), not a crash,
    so what exactly kills fsck_msdos in the all-erased case is still open.

This is a pre-existing GEMDOS filesystem-layer gap (tos-api/src/bdos/fs), not
a desktop one.  Reported as a new ticket, not fixed here.

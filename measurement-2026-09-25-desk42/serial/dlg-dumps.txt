DESK42 -- the [dlg] object dumps of the two rebuilt trees.

method/desk42_patch.py widens deskrest_patch.py's dump (which prints the
EXIT objects only) to every object: index, ob_type, ob_flags, ob_state and
the absolute rectangle with its centre point.  Read it against
references/emutos/desk/desk_rsc.c:709-768 (ADDISKIN) and :622-703
(ADFFINFO).  ob_type 20 = G_BOX, 25 = G_IBOX, 26 = G_BUTTON,
28 = G_STRING, 30 = G_FBOXTEXT; ob_flags 0x400 = FL3DBAK,
0x600 = FL3DACT, 0x200 = FL3DIND; ob_state 0x10 = OUTLINED,
0x08 = DISABLED, 0x01 = SELECTED.

The 'obj 0' line double-counts the ROOT's own position (the patch adds
o[0].ob_x to every object, and for the ROOT that is itself); the 'root'
line above it is the real one.

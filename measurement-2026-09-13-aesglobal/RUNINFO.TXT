  new dump  aes-m05-addrin/AES.BIN (5 records, 632 bytes)
  aes-m05-addrin/DIFF-vs-emutos.txt: 5 of 5 cases differ, in the six words
  that were already classified as noise -- global[2] (ap_id 0 vs 1),
  global[3]/[4] (ap_private: the SAME 4-byte LONG, big-endian on the TT and
  little-endian here, so the two half-words are swapped), global[10]
  (ap_planes 4 vs 16, a profile difference), global[11]/[12] (ap_3resv, a
  32-bit address value).  NONE of them moved.  That is the expected outcome
  and it is explainable: the words the change touches are 5..6, and the
  dumper's own `static int16_t aes_global[15]` is .bss, so those words read 0
  both before (written 0 by the 8-byte store) and after (not written).
  The AES dumper does not link gemlib -- it issues the GEM trap itself
  (tosdump/build-dumpaes-newtos.sh:10) -- so the overlay half of the change
  cannot show in this table at all.

  Serial line, identical in both runs:

  aes-m05-addrin/DIFF-vs-baseline.txt (this dump vs the 14:53 dump): ONE
  field moved, case 102 wind_get WF_NAME int_out[0] 1 -> 0.  That is NOT this
  change: it is commit 087ea43 "fix(aes): wind_get refuses WF_NAME and

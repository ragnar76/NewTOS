WHAT THE TABLE COULD NOT ASK -- see the table's own header for the citations
  * every free opcode number (16, 18, 27-29, 49, 57-69, 83-89, 92-99,
    115-119): the loader refuses them AND the ALNOFUNC alert blocks.
  * objc_sysvar 48, appl_yield 17, evnt_multi 25 (MU_TIMER/tmcount 0),
    form_error 53 (n > 63): refused by core/cases.c's TOS-3.06 premise,
    although the measured ROM reports ap_version 0340.
  * rsrc_saddr over the 17 valid R_* types: it would store the 0xDEAD poison
    through a low-memory address derived from a NULL resource header.
  * every opcode whose work is behind an addr_in pointer.

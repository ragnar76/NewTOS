# NewTOS - Why and How
- More than 20 years ago I registered newtosworld.de
- The dream: TOS on an ordinary PC - no Pi, no ARM back then
- It never got built: I couldn't program, and had no help
- This year it finally changed: I built it with Claude, an AI
- Not an emulator: TOS itself, rewritten for today's hardware
- Same GEMDOS, BIOS, XBIOS, VDI, AES - checked against EmuTOS
- Atari programs are recompiled from original sources, unchanged
- January 2026: PI-GEM, my feasibility test on the Pi
- NewTOS since May: 1,250 commits, 190,000 lines, 1,250+ tests

# What it runs on
- Raspberry Pi 4: 4 ARM cores, 1.5 GHz
  - NewTOS uses one for now; three are waiting
- Full HD over HDMI, in true colour
- The SD card is the hard disk: 10 MB/s, 32 MB RAM cache
- An ordinary USB keyboard and mouse
- Memory protection per program: a crash ends the program, not the system
- Most of the work was done in QEMU on the Mac, then on the real Pi

# BIOS and XBIOS
- The layer underneath, as a Falcon with TOS 4.04 has it
- The SD card shows up as drives C: to F:
- System variables and cookie jar where Atari software looks
- German or US keyboard layout - or your own KEYBOARD.KBD
- A 200 Hz timer, like the real machine
- A few new calls of our own: CPU clock, temperature, system info

# VDI, AES and Desktop
- The VDI draws everything - about 70 functions, in true colour
- Tested against a real Atari TT and Hatari, in several configurations
  - TT, Falcon, true colour - compared pixel by pixel
- The AES is complete: windows, menus, dialogs, events, resources
- The 3D look you know from TOS 4
- Desk accessories work - System Info is one, built just for this
- The desktop is EmuDESK, rebuilt piece by piece - TeraDesk runs on top

# Toolchain
- clang / LLVM for 64-bit ARM, plus our own SDK
- mintlib, gemlib, cflib: ported, not rewritten
- Atari sources stay untouched - fixes go into the toolchain
- Native programs: .NAP, .NCP, .NAC
  - .PRG, .TOS and .ACC stay free for a 68000 emulator
